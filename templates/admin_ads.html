1) Make the site live and compliant (required for approval)

  • Deploy publicly with HTTPS, domain, managed DB, and reliable hosting/storage.
  • Publish only rights-cleared/authorised content (no piracy/unauthorised streams or downloads).
  • Ensure legal pages (Privacy, Terms, Copyright/takedown, Contact) and ad disclosures are accurate; enable consent where required.
  • Provide good UX and genuine traffic (no incentivised clicks, no deceptive UI, no intrusive/pop-up ads that violate policies).

2) Automated in-app placements (already built)

Once enabled, ads display automatically across the site. In Settings you can turn ads On and paste approved provider code for: global/head script, homepage banner, in-content/browse slot, and movie details slot. Use responsive, policy-compliant formats.

3) Ad network (e.g. Google AdSense) — approval not guaranteed

  • Apply with your live, compliant site; meet eligibility, identity, and policy requirements.
  • After approval, paste the provider’s approved code into Settings slots. Never fabricate impressions/clicks/revenue.

4) Getting paid (Uganda) — verify official options

CineZone does not process payouts and must never collect payment credentials. Payouts come from your ad provider account based on their thresholds/schedule and officially supported methods for your account/country.

  • Check your provider’s current payout settings/docs for Uganda (available methods, verification, tax info, minimums).
  • Do not assume MTN/Airtel Mobile Money or any specific card method is supported unless the provider explicitly offers it to your account.
  • Keep informational records (provider, period, reported earnings, status, date, currency, reference) in your own bookkeeping/admin notes.

5) Other legitimate revenue

You can arrange direct, policy-compliant advertising/sponsorships with businesses (configure placements in settings). Keep records transparent and avoid misleading or intrusive ads.
