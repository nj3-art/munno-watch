Legal

Copyright & Content Removal Policy

CineZone respects intellectual property rights and distributes only content that is authorised, licensed, public domain, or otherwise legally permitted. We do not knowingly host or enable access to pirated or unauthorised copyrighted material.

Reporting concerns

If you are a rights holder or authorised agent and believe content on CineZone infringes your rights or is available without proper permission, please submit a notice including:

  • Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to be affected
  • Exact URL(s)/location(s) on CineZone
  • Your contact information and relationship/authority to act for the rights holder
  • A statement of good-faith belief that use is not authorised, and information sufficient for us to verify the claim

Our process

We will review valid notices, may temporarily restrict access pending review where appropriate, and take actions consistent with applicable law and documented rights (including removal or disabling access to unauthorised material).

Counter-notice / disputes

Where applicable, submitters may provide additional rights documentation. CineZone may request supporting evidence before restoring or publishing content.

Contact: see Contact. Process details should be finalised with qualified legal guidance for your operating jurisdictions.
